# Hotfix 5.1 - Planner Window Filters

Adds a 30 / 60 / 90 day planning-window control to Paycheck Planner.

- Default view is 30 days.
- Payment Instructions are filtered to the selected window.
- Paycheck cards are filtered to the selected window.
- Planner warnings are filtered to the selected window.
- The summary shows bills needing funding inside the selected window.
- Recurring Pay Schedules themselves remain visible because they define the household pay cadence rather than individual upcoming checks.

No scheduling math or database schema changed. This is a presentation/filtering change only.
